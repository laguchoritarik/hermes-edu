# Synthetic MP linear algebra curriculum excerpt

This is an original example for Hermes Edu tests and demonstrations. It is not an official curriculum.

## Reduction of endomorphisms

Learners should determine eigenvalues and eigenspaces of finite-dimensional linear operators, connect diagonalizability to the sum of eigenspace dimensions, and justify each implication. Exercises may progress from a diagonal matrix to a triangular matrix and then to a matrix with a repeated eigenvalue.

## Expected reasoning

A solution should show the characteristic polynomial, solve the eigenspace equations, and compare the total eigenspace dimension with the ambient dimension. For a repeated eigenvalue, algebraic multiplicity alone does not prove diagonalizability.
